## New
- Video shiurim on the Daf Yomi and the Kitzur Yomi, where one has been recorded. Tap the play button beside the daf on Home, in the Gemara, or in the reader's top bar.
- A compass toward Jerusalem in the siddur, on phones and tablets that have one. Turn it on in Settings → Reading & device, and pick the shortest route or a constant bearing.
- During the Omer, a Home card shows the blessing and tonight's count, with the sefira under it. A reminder to count at nightfall is in Settings → Notifications & learning, and skips Shabbat and Yom Tov.
- A Thursday morning heads-up when Kiddush Levana will be open by Motzei Shabbat (Settings → Notifications & learning).
- The Edot HaMizrach siddur can have a Sefardi typeface of its own. Turn on **Sefardi typeface** in the siddur's text settings.
- A bar along the top of the Gemara, Mishnah, Kitzur and Tehillim readers shows how far you are through the day's portion.
- Shnayim Mikra: tap a verse to mark your place, or hold one for reader mode, where each tap moves to the next pasuk.
- The navigation bar can hold shortcuts to Calendar, Tehillim, Learning, Alerts and Personal dates beside its tabs, or show icons only (Settings → Home & navigation).
- Settings sections fold to their headers and remember which are open. Searching opens the ones that match.
- Tehillim moved from the Siddur tab to the top of the Torah tab. Your place in the cycle is kept.
- The Siddur tab shows Quick prayers as a grid of tiles.
- Save your settings to a file and load them on another device, from Settings → Advanced → Sync between devices.
- Wear OS: a watch app that works out the date, zmanim and prayers on the watch itself, without the phone.
- Android: Google Assistant opens more prayers by name, including Musaf, Hallel, Havdalah, the Bedtime Shema and Kiddush Levana, and understands their Hebrew and Yiddish names.
- The Gemara shows the Rashbam on Bava Batra from 29a, where Rashi ends, and the Mefaresh on Tamid.
- About 250 more lines of the Koren siddur have English, and about 50 more in the Sefard.

## Fixes
- Birkat HaMazon opened after sunset hid the day's additions. They now show, with a note to say them if most of the meal was eaten before sunset.
- On narrow phones and with large text, prayer names, places and times broke mid-word or were cut short. Buttons that don't fit now move into a ⋮ menu.
- The Zmanim screen and the custom zman editor showed English in Hebrew and Yiddish.
- Setup's Sephardi spelling choice showed "Shabbos".
