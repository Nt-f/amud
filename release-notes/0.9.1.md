## New
- The Torah tab has the Gemara, Mishnah and Kitzur Shulchan Arukh, with Rashi, Tosafot and Bartenura beside the text.
- A word-by-word view puts an English word under each word of the Gemara and Mishnah. Opening one amud saves its whole tractate for offline reading.
- Daf Yomi, Mishnah Yomi and Kitzur Yomi open in the app's own reader from the Learning screen and the Home card.
- Reader mode in Tehillim, the Torah reader and the Gemara: tap for the next passage, double-tap to go back, hold to stop. Find it in the reader's top bar.
- Finishing the day's portion or a whole perek gets a short burst of confetti or fireworks.
- Fixes to the siddur texts can now arrive without an app update. They take effect the next time you open the app.
