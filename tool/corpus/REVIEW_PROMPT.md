## Part 2: Replace the spaced-number heuristic with corpus-baked verse numbers

Today `verseNumbers()` guesses verse numbers at render time from the text (`_verse` for spaced numerals such as `א הַלְלוּ יָהּ`, `_gluedVerse` for ones run into the word). Measured on the current corpus: the spaced rule fires **180** times across the five siddurim; **174** are valid-looking numerals but only **116 spaced + 1 glued = 117, in 9 leaves** (Hallel Psalms 113–118, Mizmor LeDavid, Kiddush and Zemirot for Shabbat Evening, Readings after the Service) form a real 1, 2, 3… sequence; the other 58 spaced and 2,109 glued candidates are ordinary words (Ashrei's acrostic letters are all glued and consecutive). `tool/corpus/edit.py lint` / `verses` now list exactly these. No `class="verse"` markup exists in the corpus yet, although `lib/core/html_text.dart` already styles `<sup class="verse">` and `reader_typography.dart` (`_leadingVerse`, `_verseLead`, `splitParagraphs`) already expects it.

Already built for it: `edit.py` ops `verse`/`unverse`/`balance`/`tidy`, `lint`/`verses` commands, and the formatter task `tool/corpus/agent_tasks/format.md` (`agent.py --each`). Proposal to evaluate and, if sound, specify (do not run the migration until you have shown the plan):

- **Bake** the real verse numbers into `corpus/siddur/*` as `<sup class="verse">א</sup>` once, with a one-off migration script that is conservative: only sequential runs (from א, or prev + 1 within a leaf), shown as a reviewable diff, using `tool/corpus/edit.py` proposals so a human approves them. Include the glued form (`יבמָה אָשִׁיב`) and the Sefard/Edot/Koren/Chabad texts, not only Ashkenaz.
- **Delete** the render-time guessing (`verseNumbers`, `_verse`, `_gluedVerse`) from the reader so text is shown as the corpus says, and update `test/reading_marks_test.dart` / `test/typeset_test.dart` fixtures (psalm116 uses a glued numeral).
- **Guard** it: a validator rule in `tool/corpus/source.py` that verse `<sup>` numbers in a part are valid numerals and consecutive, and that unmarked numerals-looking tokens are not left behind (warn); an editor affordance to add or remove a verse number; keep the "verse numbers" setting in Tehillim working (`lib/features/tehillim/*` has its own path; check it is unaffected).
- Answer: does any other consumer depend on the heuristic (search normalisation in `lib/core/search.dart`, voice matching, `hebrewWords`/`stripHtml` that must now skip the `<sup>`)? Is the 58 count right, and what do the other 120 matches look like? What would be lost for Sefaria-sourced alternative versions, which are not baked?
