## New
- The Gemara page view sets each amud line for line as the Vilna Shas prints it, where the line maps are ready. Other amudim are laid out by rule as before.
- Search finds a masechta or daf by number, like "Berachos 2a", "Shabbos 31b" or "ברכות ב:", and a perek of the Mishnah, like "Avos 3". It also finds the parshiyos and other names people use for the Torah tab's books.
- Search finds prayer text by its words, in any order.
- Editing Home shows the cards as they look, hidden ones too. Tapping one opens its settings under a live preview.
- The Torah tab has a **Download everything** button, with the size of what isn't saved yet and a way to cancel.
- Learning progress, Shnayim Mikra and Tehillim can sync between devices and go into settings files (Settings → Advanced → Sync between devices → Progress). Marks from different devices add up.
- The navigation bar fits as many tabs and shortcuts as the screen has room for.
- iOS: San Francisco text at Apple's sizes, the glass tab bar on iOS 26, native alerts, and each settings group in one card.

## Fixes
- The siddur's compass pointed toward Jerusalem. It now points east.
- Kaddish headings in Koren, Sefard and Edot HaMizrach weren't grouped with their Kaddish, so the reader could label it wrongly.
- "The chazzan says:" was shown twice, once as the siddur's rubric and once as Amud's label.
- Opening a prayer from Home left it open on the Siddur tab afterwards. The tab now opens on its first screen.
- The warm page color is a little more like yellowed paper.
