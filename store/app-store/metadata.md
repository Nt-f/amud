## Description (4000)

Amud is a free, offline siddur that follows the day. Ya'aleh VeYavo, Hallel, Al HaNissim, Tachanun and the Omer show only when they're said, so you daven from the page without flipping or guessing.

SIDDUR
• Ashkenaz, Sefard, Edot HaMizrach, Chabad and Koren, all included and all offline
• Hebrew and English side by side, line by line, or alone
• A bar of the key points in Shacharis, Mincha and Maariv: Shema, Shemoneh Esrei, Hallel, the Torah reading
• "Why today?" explains every addition and omission, and lets you preview any date
• "Prepare for tomorrow" shows what changes in the next day's davening
• Birchas Hamazon for a bris, sheva brachos or pidyon haben, Me'ein Shalosh, Tefillas HaDerech, Krias Shema al HaMitah and more
• Hoshanos, Selichos, Chanukah, Hakafos and other holiday tefillos in one place
• About 40 Hebrew fonts, and Ashkenazi spelling for English (Shabbos, Shacharis) if you want it

ZMANIM AND CALENDAR
• Every zman for where you are, by the GRA, Magen Avraham or Baal HaTanya, with your own custom zmanim
• Candle lighting and havdalah
• A Jewish calendar with Yamim Tovim, parshiyos and Hebrew dates
• Reminders before any zman, like candle lighting or the latest Shema

TORAH
• The Gemara page as the Vilna Shas prints it, with Rashi and Tosafos, or elucidated in English, side by side, phrase by phrase or word by word
• Mishnah with Bartenura, and the Kitzur Shulchan Aruch with today's Kitzur Yomi
• Daily learning: Daf Yomi, Mishnah Yomi, Rambam, Yerushalmi, Chofetz Chaim and more
• Tehillim by the month or the week, Shir shel Yom, and your progress saved
• Shiurim: kezayis, revi'is, amah and the rest by each posek, with a converter and sources

YOUR HOME SCREEN
• A dashboard of cards you arrange: the Hebrew date and parsha, what changes today, the next zman, Sefirat HaOmer while it's counted, quick prayers, a minyan finder and notes
• Widgets, and an Apple Watch app with the date, the parsha and the next zman
• English, Hebrew and Yiddish interface

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
Every siddur text and font ships with the app. Your location is used on the device to work out zmanim and is never sent anywhere. Torah books are a one-time download from Sefaria and then work offline.

Texts come from Sefaria; the calendar and zmanim from a full port of Hebcal.
