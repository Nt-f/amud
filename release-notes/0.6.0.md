## New
- **Search.** Home has a search bar that looks through everything: prayers in every siddur, Tehillim by chapter ("Tehillim 23", "תהלים כג"), zmanim with today's times, Torah books and simanim, the app's pages and settings. The Siddur, Torah, Zmanim and Settings tabs have their own search, and a downloaded Torah book can be searched from its page, including its full text. Hebrew searches ignore nikud and te'amim, and English searches accept any common spelling: "shacharis" finds Shacharit and "birchas hamazon" finds Birkat HaMazon.
- **Swipe to turn the page or change tabs.** Swipe sideways across the text to go to the next or previous section of the siddur, siman in the Torah reader, or psalm when reading a single psalm of Tehillim. On the first screen of each tab, the same swipe moves to the next or previous tab. With a Hebrew interface, swipes go the other way, like a Hebrew book. Swipes that start at the edge of the screen are left for the back gesture.
- **Jump bar in every service.** Open Shacharis, Mincha or Maariv from anywhere and a bar under the title jumps to its key points, such as Shema, Shemoneh Esrei, Hallel and the Torah reading. It follows along as you scroll. Parts said today that are printed elsewhere in the siddur, such as Musaf or the day's Hoshana, open from the bar too.
- **Anonymous usage statistics.** Amud now shares which screens, features, prayers and texts are used, to help decide what to improve. It never sends what you type, your name or your exact location, and it gets a new anonymous ID each time it starts, so nothing is linked to you. Turn it off under Settings → Advanced → Share anonymous usage.

## Improved
- The Today card on Home is redesigned. Shacharis, Mincha and Maariv are large buttons, with the one for this time of day marked "Now". The day's other prayers are listed below, with a dot on the ones for today. What's added or not said today is listed in words.
- The separate *Today's davening* screen is gone. Its jump bar is now part of each service in the reader.
- The opening animation waits until the app is on screen, so it no longer plays partly hidden behind the launch screen.
- When several new features are announced at once, the "What's new" card scrolls instead of pushing the rest of Home off the screen.
- Calendar days scale down to fit on small screens instead of overflowing.
- The update banner now says "Amud" instead of "Siddur".
