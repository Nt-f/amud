## New
- Shnayim Mikra on the Torah tab: the week's parsha an aliyah a day, each verse twice and then Targum Onkelos, in the siddur's fonts and layout.
- Shnayim Mikra can show Rashi under each verse, in Rashi script, with or without nikud, and in English. Choose and drag into order what's shown in its text settings.
- Read Shnayim Mikra a verse at a time, a paragraph at a time, or a whole aliyah at a time.
- Check off each aliyah as you finish it. "Your year" shows every parsha of the year, and the Home screen shows today's aliyah.
- Shnayim Mikra and Rashi download from Sefaria once, then work offline.
- Shiurim, on the Torah tab: kezayis, revi'is, amah, techum Shabbos and more as each posek ruled them, with sources, and a converter between Gemara units and metric or imperial.
- Settings → Offline & storage lists what's downloaded and the room each takes. "Download all texts" gets everything before a trip or Yom Tov.
- Short notes appear on the days they matter, like what to do if you forgot Mashiv HaRuach in the 30 days after Shmini Atzeres.
- The Zmanim screen's sky follows the time of day: the sun crosses it by day, and the moon, in its phase, crosses it at night among the stars.
- The five main siddurim now use Amud's own edited text, listed first as "Amud" in the version list, with the Sefaria versions it came from credited.
- Settings → About links to the privacy policy.

## Improved
- In Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers, the web app runs as WebAssembly, and it downloads about a quarter less to work offline.
- Tap anywhere to skip the opening animation, in the app and on the web.
- A custom card that can't update, for example offline, shows its last result with "Not updated since …" instead of an error.
- The voice model download picks up where it stopped instead of starting over.
