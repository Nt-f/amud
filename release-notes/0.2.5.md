## New
- **In-app updates on Android.** Tap *Update now* and the app downloads and installs the new version. You no longer need the browser. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from Siddur.
- **What's new.** After an update, Home shows a banner that links to these notes.
- **Short notes.** Halachic notes now appear only when they apply today, and they are brief: for example, "Say Ya'aleh VeYavo" on Rosh Chodesh, or Mashiv HaRuach in the first 30 days. Turn this off under Text settings to see the siddur's full notes.

## Improved
- The Home screen no longer has a top bar, so the cards start at the top. *Edit dashboard* is now below the cards.
- When editing the dashboard, each card has a ⋮ menu, and tapping a card configures it.
- In *Hide* mode, the options not said today in Ya'aleh VeYavo and similar passages are crossed out. The option that is said is highlighted.
- Al HaNissim, Aneinu, Nachem, Atah Chonantanu and the Aseret Yemei Teshuvah additions are hidden on days they aren't said, in every nusach.
- Instructions like "On regular weekdays continue…" no longer show when the highlight already says so.
- Titles, choices and menus fit on narrow phones and with large text, instead of breaking words mid-letter.

## Fixed
- Ya'aleh VeYavo in Nusach Sefard is highlighted in full.
