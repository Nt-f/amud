## Fixes
- From their second launch, the phone and desktop apps went back to October's siddur text, which hid "Ve'al kulam" in the Sefard weekday Amidah. Amud now takes a text update only when it is newer than its own.
- On slower phones, opening a siddur no longer freezes the screen, and scrolling a long service is smoother.
- A stray swipe on Home no longer moves to another tab.
- In a service with its parts along the top, like Shacharit, a sideways swipe no longer turns to the next prayer by mistake.
- On a flip phone, the down key reads through long text a step at a time instead of jumping past it to the next button.
- On a flip phone, the opening logo was cut off or gone before it was drawn. It now fits the screen and stays long enough to see.

## New
- A text report now names the lines of the siddur's text you selected and the version of Amud you read them in, so mistakes are quicker to find.
- On a flip phone, the left soft key's menu lists the page's own choices and its app bar's tools first, then every tab under "Go to".
- In Shacharit on a flip phone, left and right go to the next or previous part of the service, as the bar along the top lists them.
- On a flip phone, left and right turn Tehillim's tabs.
