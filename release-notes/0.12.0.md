## New
- The Gemara page view now sets nearly every amud in Shas line for line as the Vilna Shas prints it. A few amudim are still laid out by rule.
- On Mondays, Thursdays and Shabbos afternoon, the Torah reading shows its three aliyos, Kohen, Levi and Yisrael, in a printed frame.
- A small tefillin mark with יד or ראש shows at the words where you touch the tefillin.
- The reader's jump bar can list every section, not only the main ones.
- Seasonal wording like Morid HaTal and Mashiv HaRuach is highlighted for its first 30 days, in the summer as well as the winter.
- Thousands more lines in the Ashkenaz, Sefard, Koren, Chabad and Edot HaMizrach siddurim have an English translation.
- The launch animation is over in well under a second.

## Fixes
- The siddur's compass pointed east. It now points to the Western Wall from your saved location, and the compass sheet says how many degrees from north that is.
- Chabad showed Tachanun on its own days without it, like the rest of Tishrei, 19 Kislev and 12–13 Tammuz. Vihi Noam was said before a week with Yom Tov.
- Lines said only on some days, like Aneinu, Nachem, Avinu Malkeinu and the Ten Days changes, showed every day in several siddurim. Other lines that are said were missing.
- In Koren, more than 100 prayers were shown as instructions, so their Hebrew was hidden. The zimun and Ya'aleh VeYavo in bentching were missing.
- Edot HaMizrach showed Rosh HaShana, Ushpizin and Sukkos texts on every day of Chol HaMoed.
- Me'ein Shalosh on a flip phone was shrunk until it couldn't be read. It now scrolls at your reader's text size.
