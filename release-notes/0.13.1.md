## New
- The Sefard siddur's weekday Shacharis, Mincha and Maariv now have a section for each prayer, as the Ashkenaz siddur does. The blessing titles are centered, and Ashrei, Aleinu and each part of the Shema are in the jump bar.
- Before the Amidah, the siddur shows when to take three steps back and forward, and "Adonai Sefasai Tiftach" has its own line.
- The Sefard Shema and Ashrei mark where to touch the tefillin and kiss the tzitzis, and Pesukei DeZimra marks where to stand.
- In Shnayim Mikra, a long press on a day's chip checks that aliyah off or clears it. Only days you checked off show a check.

## Fixes
- In Sefard, the end of Modim ("Ve'al kulam") was missing on ordinary days, and Shomer Yisrael and the Kaddish after Tachanun were missing except on Mondays and Thursdays.
- Sefard Mincha said Avinu Malkeinu after Aleinu, and Shacharis said it after Tachanun. It now comes right after the Amidah.
- On Rosh Chodesh and Chol HaMoed, Sefard Shacharis now returns the Torah before the Half Kaddish and Musaf, and the end of Shacharis isn't shown twice on Chol HaMoed.
- Sefard Maariv counts the Omer before Aleinu, says V'Atah Kadosh on Purim and Tisha B'Av nights, and has "Oseh HaShalom" in the Ten Days.
- Hallel: the whole Hallel on Shemini Atzeres and Simchas Torah, and none on Erev Chanukah or Pesach Sheni.
- The note about starting "V'sein tal umatar" never appeared. It now shows for the first month.
- The compass no longer clicks when it points to the Western Wall.
