## New
- Amud works on flip phones with no touch screen. The arrow keys move and scroll, the left soft key opens a menu, the right one goes back, and a bar at the bottom names them.
- On a flip phone, the siddur scrolls with up and down, and left and right turn to the next prayer. The center key hides the bars, `*` and `#` change the text size, and `0` goes to the top.
- Keypad navigation turns on by itself on phones without a touch screen. To turn it on or off yourself, go to Settings → Home & navigation → **Keypad navigation**.
- A text report from the siddur now says which section and lines you selected and the day you read them, so mistakes are quicker to find and fix.

## Fixes
- In Sefard Birkat HaMazon, the notes for special days showed every day, the zimun said "our God" without ten, and the ending at a bris was printed twice.
- Al Naharot now comes on days with Tachanun and Shir HaMaalot on days without, in Sefard and Ashkenaz Birkat HaMazon.
- The Sefard Shabbat Amidah showed Al HaNissim and Ya'aleh VeYavo every Shabbat, and Sim Shalom and Modim were missing from the silent Amidah.
- In the Sefard Haggadah, Retzei showed on weeknights. Every line of Barech now has English.
- On a small screen, setup's Back and Next buttons no longer run off the edge.

## Removed
- Explain this line is gone from the siddur's selection menu.
