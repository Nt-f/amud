## New
- **A siddur that knows every line.** Every line of the Ashkenaz, Sefard, Edot HaMizrach, Chabad and Koren siddurim has been read and marked: what is a prayer and what is an instruction, which days it is said, and which lines are alternatives to each other. What Amud shows today now comes from that, not from guessing at the formatting, so far fewer lines are wrongly shown or left out.
- **Who says what.** A small line above a prayer tells you how it is read: Chazzan, Cong., "Cong., then chazzan", Together with the chazzan, Mourner, said in an undertone, bow, three steps back, ark opened, ×3 and more. The congregation's answers inside a line, like "Amen" in Kaddish, sit on their own line, marked "Cong.".
- **"If…" lines.** Lines that depend on you rather than the calendar are shown with a small label instead of being guessed: "If: Ten or more ate together", "If: For a man", "If not: At a festive meal".
- **Today's davening in the right order.** The day's additions now come in the order they are said, in every siddur. Musaf on Rosh Chodesh and Chol HaMoed comes after Ashrei and Uva LeTziyon, Selichot on a fast come right after Shemoneh Esrei, and Hoshanot come after Hallel, or after Musaf in Nusach Ashkenaz, opening the day's Hoshana. Fast-day Mincha has its Torah reading. After Maariv come the Omer, Kiddush Levana and, on Motzaei Shabbat, Havdalah. Shabbos and Yom Tov work as before.
- **Widgets and watches.** Add Amud to your home screen on Android, iPhone and Mac, with the date, the next zman and the Omer. Android phones also have a Wear OS companion with a tile, complications and a small offline siddur: Tefillat HaDerech, Birkat HaMazon and the bedtime Shema. An Apple Watch companion is included for iPhone.
- **Shortcuts and links.** Pick up to four prayers for the app icon's menu, and on Android pin any prayer to the home screen. Siri and Shortcuts can open a prayer or tell you the next zman and tonight's Omer. Links like `amud://pray/derech` or `amud.page/app/siddur/ashkenaz/mincha` open straight to a prayer.
- **Do Not Disturb while reading (Android).** Turn it on under Settings → Reading & device, and Amud silences calls and notifications in the siddur and restores them when you leave. It never overrides a Do Not Disturb or Focus mode you already have on.
- **Personal dates.** Save yahrzeits, birthdays and bar or bat mitzvah dates by their Hebrew date, with reminders the evening before or that morning. Leap years and short months are handled, and a bar mitzvah shows its parsha.
- **Kiddush Levana and Birkat HaChama cards.** A card shows the Kiddush Levana window, sits under Sefirat HaOmer, and by default appears only while the window is open. You can add optional reminders during the window. A Birkat HaChama card shows the next blessing of the sun.
- **Card gallery.** Browse ready-made custom cards and install them, or load a community collection from a link. Each card's code is shown and previewed before you install it.
- **On the desktop.** A tray icon, and in the reader keys 1–9 jump to the parts of the service while Page Up and Page Down turn the page.
- **In the web app.** Share text or links to Amud to save them as a note card. The Omer badge shows on the app icon until you mark tonight's count, and where the server supports it, reminders can arrive while the app is closed.
- **New custom:** Tefillin on Chol HaMoed (Settings → Customs). It's off by default, so Tefillin are left out on Chol HaMoed.

## Improved
- "Hide" now really hides what isn't said today, including the alternative wordings that used to stay crossed out. Hide is the new default, as is showing instructions and notes in English only.
- Labels like "Not on Tisha B'Av" are gone from prayers said every ordinary day. A label now appears only when it tells you something about today, and a line left out today says why, for example "Not on Chol HaMoed".
- Instructions shown in English alone line up on the right, with the Hebrew they belong to.
- Hallel reads more clearly: verse numbers are faint, and the responsive lines of Hodu and Ana Hashem each start their own line.
- Opening a section on a day none of it is said shows what's left out, instead of a blank page.
- The screen stays on while you read, by default.
- Dashboard cards that only show sometimes, like Sefirat HaOmer or the Kiddush Levana window, say so when you add or edit them, along with whether they're hidden right now.
- The opening animation is about half a second shorter.
- Search finds prayers by their other names and shows results from every siddur.
- Custom siddur rules have a builder and presets beside the JSON editor.

## Fixed
- Many lines that were shown on the wrong days or left out, among them:
  - Morid HaTal was shown in the summer for Ashkenaz outside Israel.
  - Hallel was left out on Chol HaMoed, Chanukah and Yom Tov whenever it was printed only under Rosh Chodesh.
  - Tal was shown on the seventh day of Pesach.
  - In Edot HaMizrach, the weekday Shemoneh Esrei was hidden outside the Ten Days of Repentance.
  - In Sefard, Tachanun was inverted.
  - In Koren, the weekday Shema was hidden when davening without a minyan.
  - Avinu Malkeinu now follows its real days.
- Mourner's Kaddish and Kaddish d'Rabbanan are marked as the mourner's, and the aliyah blessings as the one called up, instead of the chazzan's.
- Birkas Kohanim inside Shemoneh Esrei is marked as part of the chazzan's repetition.
- Chanukah candles no longer appear twice in Maariv.
- Dashboard cards in a row no longer overflow when their content loads after the row is laid out.
