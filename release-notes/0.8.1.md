## New
- Birkat Hamazon folds the zimun, Al Naharot and Shir HaMaalot into one row each. Tap a row to open it.
- Birkat Hamazon asks whose table you ate at (your own, your parents' or someone else's) and shows only that blessing. This is in the Ashkenaz, Sefard, Edot HaMizrach and Koren siddurim.
- A "Special occasion" row at the top of Birkat Hamazon lets you pick a bris, wedding or pidyon haben. It changes the psalm, and in Edot HaMizrach and Sefard it also shows the matching blessings.
- Every Kaddish is now one card, wherever it appears in the service.
- Birkat Hamazon now has English for the lines that had none, in all five siddurim.
- Hebrew and Yiddish for 72 more menu and settings texts.

## Improved
- Choosing an option inside the text no longer sends you back to the top of the page.
- The Ashkenazi spelling setting covers more words, such as Naharos and Moshiach.
- The Linux app shows the Amud icon.

## Fixed
- Sefard, Koren and Edot HaMizrach showed the Song of the Day for all six days at once. They show only today's.
- Many prayers were missing in Hebrew because they were marked as instructions: Chabad's Shema blessings, Koren's Kaddish, Kedushah and Barchu lines, and several Amidah additions in Koren, Chabad and Edot HaMizrach. They show now.
- Chabad's Kaddish left out a few words after each Amen.
- Edot HaMizrach showed all of Avinu Malkeinu in the weekday Amidah on ordinary days.
- The weekday Amidah in Ashkenaz showed Kedushah as two separate Chazaras HaShatz rows. It is one row now.
