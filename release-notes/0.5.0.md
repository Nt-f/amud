## New
- **Jump through today's davening.** A bar under the title of *Today's davening* jumps straight to each service and its key points: Shema, Shemoneh Esrei, Hallel, the Torah reading, Musaf, Aleinu and more. It follows along as you scroll.
- **Reading mode for Torah.** Double-tap the text in the Torah reader to hide the bars, as in the siddur. Double-tap again to bring them back.
- **Custom cards can use the internet.** Cards you write in JavaScript can now fetch data from the web (https only), and have up to 15 seconds to run. A guide is in the app's repository at `skills/js-card/SKILL.md`.
- **More font files.** Import OpenType and TrueType fonts, font collections (.ttc) and WOFF fonts. On Android and iPhone, the file picker now shows every file, so fonts saved as plain downloads can be chosen.

## Improved
- Downloaded books no longer show a large "Available offline" box. A small icon in the top bar shows it instead, with the space used and an option to remove the download.
- The Kitzur Yomi card and today's highlighted se'ifim use the app's blue instead of pink.
- Birkat HaMazon no longer shows its title twice in Nusach Sefard. In the weekday Sefard siddur, the Birkat HaMazon shortcut opens the everyday text rather than the Sheva Berachos one.
- The opening animation always plays in full.

## Fixed
- A setting that couldn't be read after an update no longer resets all your settings. Only that one setting goes back to its default.
